Gentamicin (traditional dosing) - patient and regimen

Model: ke from the selected equation; Vd = 0.25 L/kg (IBW, or adjusted weight if > 120% IBW); infusion 0.5 h. Confirm targets with the local protocol. Extended-interval dosing is not covered.

Digoxin (adult, steady-state) - patient and regimen

Dosing weight = IBW (actual weight if lower). Vd = 3.8·wt + 3.1·CrCl. F: IV 1.0, tablet 0.7, elixir 0.8, capsule 0.9. Levels must be drawn at least 6 h (ideally 8-24 h, pre-dose) after a dose and at steady state. Low K or Mg, hypercalcemia, hypothyroidism and hypoxia increase toxicity risk. Targets: HF about 0.5-0.9, AF about 0.8-1.2 ng/mL; confirm with local protocol.

Theophylline / Aminophylline (adult) - patient and regimen

Dosing weight = IBW (actual if lower). Vd = 0.5 L/kg; CL = 0.04 L/kg/h × factors (smoker ×1.6, heart failure ×0.5, cirrhosis ×0.5, clearance-lowering drug ×0.6, enzyme inducer ×1.6). Aminophylline = 0.8 theophylline. Target 5-15 mg/L (10-15 for most benefit); above 20 toxicity rises and kinetics become non-linear. Dose from IBW; clearance varies widely between similar patients, so confirm with a level.

Phenytoin (adult, oral) - patient and regimen

Nonlinear (Michaelis-Menten) kinetics: at steady state F·D = Vmax·C/(Km + C). Population Vmax = 7 mg/kg/day (x 0.75 if hepatic disease), Km = 4 mg/L; dosing weight = IBW if above 120% IBW; Vd = 0.7 L/kg. Salt factor S (bioavailability taken as 1): capsule and IV 0.92, chewable tablet and suspension 1.0; the IV loading dose is divided by 0.92. Therapeutic total 10-20 mg/L (free 1-2 mg/L); levels are pre-dose, after 7-14 days on a stable dose. Correct the level for low albumin (Sheiner-Tozer) before adjusting. Phenytoin is not removed significantly by dialysis; use free levels in renal failure. Phenytoin sodium contains about 92% phenytoin acid.

Tacrolimus (adult) - patient and regimen

First-order kinetics: at steady state the trough is proportional to the dose, so the dose is adjusted linearly (new = old x target/measured, capped at +/-50% per step). Label initial doses (adult, PO divided q12h): kidney 0.1 mg/kg/day with MMF/IL-2 antagonist (0.2 with azathioprine), liver 0.10-0.15, heart 0.075; lower with hepatic disease or an azole/macrolide. Doses are rounded to whole mg/day. Trough: pre-dose, at steady state (about 3-5 days; longer with hepatic disease). Tacrolimus is metabolized hepatically - no renal dose adjustment, but it is nephrotoxic: monitor creatinine, K and Mg. Watch QT and glucose.

Cyclosporine (adult) - patient and regimen

First-order kinetics: linear dose adjustment (new = old x target/measured, capped at +/-50% per step). Label initial doses (Neoral, divided q12h): kidney 9 +/- 3, liver 8 +/- 4, heart 7 +/- 3, RA/psoriasis 2.5 mg/kg/day; lower with hepatic disease or an interacting drug. Doses are rounded to 50 mg/day (25 mg per dose). Trough pre-dose at steady state (2-4 days). Modified and original forms are NOT interchangeable. Nephrotoxic: monitor creatinine, BP, potassium, magnesium, lipids; gingival hyperplasia with long use.

Linezolid (adult) - patient and regimen

Standard dosing: 600 mg q12h IV or PO (oral bioavailability about 100%). No automatic renal dose adjustment per manufacturer; renal impairment warrants CBC monitoring and individualized specialist/TDM review. Linezolid is removed by dialysis: dose after the session. TDM target trough 2-8 mg/L. Watch thrombocytopenia (and anemia), lactic acidosis, peripheral/optic neuropathy beyond 28 days, and serotonin syndrome with serotonergic drugs.

Fluconazole (adult) - patient and regimen

Weight-based dosing (actual weight): invasive candidiasis: load 12 mg/kg (max 800) then 6 mg/kg (max 400) q24h; cryptococcal induction: 1200 mg q24h without a load (WHO 2022; IDSA at least 800); oesophageal: 200 mg then 100 mg q24h. If CrCl is 50 or below (and not on dialysis) halve the maintenance dose (loading unchanged). Fluconazole is removed by hemodialysis: give the dose after the session; PD needs level/protocol-guided dosing. Watch QT, liver enzymes and CYP2C9/3A4 interactions (warfarin, sulfonylureas...).
About, version history and limitations
Intended use: decision support for clinical pharmacists and physicians: dose calculation, level prediction and level-based adjustment for adult patients (18+). It does not replace clinical judgement or hospital protocol.
Limits common to all modules: adults only; population-based models with individual fitting only when levels are entered; levels must be at steady state and correctly timed; dialysis/CRRT is blocked unless stated; every result needs pharmacist review; not clinically validated; confirm targets and doses with the local protocol.
